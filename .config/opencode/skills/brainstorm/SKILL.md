## What I do

- Guide structured brainstorming sessions with clear phases
- Generate diverse ideas without premature judgment
- Apply creative thinking techniques (mind mapping, SCAMPER, reverse brainstorming)
- Organize and categorize ideas by theme or feasibility
- Evaluate options using weighted criteria
- Synthesize findings into actionable next steps

## When to use me

Use this skill when you need to:
- Explore multiple approaches to a technical problem
- Generate feature ideas for a product
- Find creative solutions when stuck on implementation
- Evaluate trade-offs between different architectural decisions
- Plan a new project or major refactor

## Brainstorming process

### Phase 1: Define the challenge
1. Clarify the problem statement or goal
2. Identify constraints and requirements
3. Establish success criteria

### Phase 2: Diverge (generate ideas)
1. Suspend judgment - all ideas are valid initially
2. Aim for quantity over quality
3. Build on previous ideas
4. Encourage wild ideas - they often spark practical ones

### Phase 3: Organize
1. Group similar ideas into themes
2. Identify patterns and connections
3. Remove duplicates while preserving nuance

### Phase 4: Converge (evaluate)
1. Apply evaluation criteria (feasibility, impact, effort, risk)
2. Score or rank options
3. Identify quick wins vs long-term investments

### Phase 5: Synthesize
1. Summarize top candidates
2. Outline concrete next steps
3. Document decisions and rationale

## Output format

I will provide:
1. **Ideas list**: All generated ideas with brief descriptions
2. **Grouped themes**: Ideas organized by category
3. **Evaluation matrix**: Top ideas scored against criteria
4. **Recommendations**: Prioritized list with rationale
5. **Next steps**: Concrete actions to move forward

## Tips for effective sessions

- Start with a clear, specific question
- Share relevant context about constraints
- Be open to unexpected directions
- Let me know your evaluation priorities (speed, quality, cost, etc.)
